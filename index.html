Roadworthy

Roadworthy keeps track of the hours a new driver practices with an adult in the car. You start it when the drive begins and stop it when the drive ends. It saves everything for you.

It works on Android phones only. It does not work on iPhones.

1. Put it on your phone

Open this page on your phone, then tap the button.

Download Roadworthy

This always gets the newest version.

  1. Your phone might say the file could be harmful. It is safe. You see this because the app is not in the Play Store. Tap Download anyway.
  2. When it finishes, tap Open. (Or pull down from the top of the screen and tap the download.)
  3. Your phone might say it can't install apps from here. Tap Settings, turn on the switch, then go back.
  4. Tap Install.
  5. If a warning says unsafe app or unknown app, tap More details, then Install anyway.

Now Roadworthy is with your other apps.

2. The first time you open it

  1. Tap New here? Create an account. Type your email, make up a password, and tap Create account.
  2. We send you an email. Open it and tap the link inside.

    Don't see the email? Wait a few minutes, then look in your Spam or Junk folder.

  3. Go back to Roadworthy. Fill in your name, your birthday and your state, tick the boxes, and tap Continue.
  4. Did Dave send you a code? Tap Join with a code and type it in. Now you can see the driver.

3. Log a drive

  1. Tap Start drive before you leave.
  2. Drive. You can put the phone away.
  3. When you get home, open Roadworthy and tap Stop drive.
  4. Answer the questions about the drive and tap Save drive.

Bring in your hours from RoadReady

Did you already log drives in the RoadReady app? You can move them into Roadworthy so you don't lose them.

First, in RoadReady:

  1. Open RoadReady and go to the driver's Driving Log tab.
  2. Tap Export Driving Report. The report opens.
  3. Tap the download button to save it to your phone.
  4. Watch for a message that says where it was saved. It's usually the Downloads folder.

Then, in Roadworthy:

  1. At the top, tap the menu and pick the driver.
  2. At the bottom, tap Driver.
  3. Tap Import hours from RoadReady.
  4. Tap the report. Look in Recent or Downloads. It's the newest file.
  5. Roadworthy shows how many drives and hours it found. Check that it matches RoadReady, then tap Import.

If the driver's name is spelled differently in RoadReady, the import won't work. Ask Dave.
Doing it twice is fine. Drives that are already there won't be added again.

New versions

When there's a new version, Roadworthy says so at the top of the screen. Tap Update.

The first time, your phone asks if Roadworthy can install apps. Turn on the switch, go back, and tap Update again. Then tap Install. Your drives are kept.
